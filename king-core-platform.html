King - Core Platform: Internal Platform UX Improvement

Principal UX Designer · Core Platform · 2022-Present

I led UX strategy across King's internal Core Platform to improve how game teams discover, access, and manage platform capabilities. The work focused on turning disconnected internal tools into clearer, more self-service workflows grounded in research, shared mental models, and reusable platform patterns.

Challenge
Core Platform teams supported many critical workflows, but the user experience was fragmented across tools, permissions, workspace setup, environment management, and support channels.

Outcome
The work created clearer UX direction, reusable research practices, self-service patterns, and a stronger platform vision for internal users and product teams.

The Strategic Challenge

A powerful platform that felt fragmented to its users

Game teams relied on Core Platform for essential production workflows, but the experience required too much coordination, context switching, and prior knowledge.

1

Disconnected entry points

Users had to move across multiple tools and support channels to understand what existed, what applied to their game, and what action to take next.

2

Low self-service confidence

Workspace, access, and environment flows depended on manual coordination because users could not always see system state, ownership, or safe next steps.

3

No shared UX language

Teams were solving related problems with different mental models, making it hard to scale quality across the platform.

The design challenge was to make the platform feel coherent, predictable, and usable without requiring users to understand the organization behind it.

My Role

I worked as the Principal UX Designer across discovery, strategy, synthesis, stakeholder alignment, and product direction. My contribution was not limited to interface design: I helped define how the organization should understand platform users, prioritize problems, and translate research into reusable product patterns.

Scope
Platform

Workspace management, IAM, environment management, research operations, and platform hub strategy.

Users
Game Teams

Developers, tech leads, operations, workspace owners, and adjacent internal platform users.

Method
Research-led

Interviews, journey mapping, synthesis, workshops, concept validation, and requirements framing.

Research & Synthesis

I established a clearer insight loop for Core Platform by collecting user evidence, turning scattered feedback into structured themes, and connecting findings to planning conversations.

  • Mapped user workflows across workspace setup, access management, environment management, and platform discovery.
  • Identified where users relied on Slack, manual support, or individual knowledge instead of self-service product flows.
  • Built reusable research templates and helped teams use Dovetail as a more centralized insight repository.
  • Connected research findings to roadmap discussions, OKRs, and product prioritization.

Design Direction

1. Make ownership and state visible
Users needed to understand who owned a workspace, what access existed, what state an environment was in, and what was safe to change.
2. Move repeated tasks into self-service flows
The platform needed to reduce support dependency by giving workspace owners clearer actions, guardrails, and confirmation states.
3. Create reusable platform patterns
Similar workflows needed consistent structures for setup, configuration, review, troubleshooting, and recovery.
4. Connect tools through a platform mental model
Users should experience Core Platform as a connected product ecosystem, not a set of unrelated internal tools.

Key Workstreams

Workspace & IAM Self-Service

I defined journeys and requirements for workspace owners so they could manage access and permissions with less manual coordination, clearer confirmation, and more confidence.

Environment Management

I helped simplify how developers and QA understood available environments, configuration state, and troubleshooting paths, balancing common workflows with advanced needs.

Centralized Platform Experience

I proposed and validated a more unified platform hub concept to improve discoverability, reduce context switching, and give game teams a clearer starting point for platform tasks.

UX Maturity & Research Operations

I introduced research rituals, templates, insight structures, and practical UX principles that helped product teams make more evidence-led decisions even without dedicated UX support.

Impact

  • Improved strategic clarity for Core Platform UX
    Created a shared language for platform quality, self-service, discoverability, and coherent internal workflows.
  • Reduced dependency on manual coordination
    Moved key workflows toward clearer productized paths, helping users act with more confidence and fewer support handoffs.
  • Scaled research practices across teams
    Introduced templates, evidence structures, and insight repositories that made research easier to reuse in planning.
  • Established reusable platform patterns
    Turned recurring problems into patterns that could be applied across workspace, IAM, environment, and platform hub experiences.

Key Takeaway

The work reframed Core Platform UX from a collection of separate product problems into an ecosystem challenge. The most important design opportunity was not only improving individual screens, but helping teams create a platform experience users could understand, trust, and operate independently.
