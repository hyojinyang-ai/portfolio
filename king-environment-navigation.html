Case 04 / Platform Experience Simplification

Environment Manager & Navigation Redesign

Principal UX Designer | Internal Platform UX | 2023-Present

I worked on two connected platform problems: helping teams understand and manage environments more confidently, and redesigning navigation so complex internal tooling felt less fragmented.

User Problem

Users had to jump between tools and interpret technical states without enough context, making everyday platform operations slower and riskier.

Design Goal

Reduce context switching through clearer information architecture, visible status, and task-oriented navigation patterns.

Problem

The platform had grown through many tools and teams. For users, this created a fragmented experience: navigation reflected internal system boundaries, not their mental model, and environment-related workflows lacked enough status and relationship context.

The result was operational uncertainty. Users had to ask whether an environment was safe to act on, which tool owned a task, and where they should go next.

Environment Manager

What I focused on

  • Mapping how teams understood environment state, ownership, and dependencies.
  • Clarifying the difference between viewing status, managing configuration, and taking action.
  • Designing flows that surfaced the right context before users made operational changes.

Design direction

The experience moved toward a status-first model: users should understand what an environment is, what state it is in, who owns it, and what actions are available before they commit to changes.

Navigation redesign

What changed

The navigation work reorganized platform entry points around user tasks and product areas rather than technical ownership. It also made repeated patterns easier to recognize across the platform.

Key decisions

  • Group related platform tasks so users do not need to understand internal team boundaries.
  • Keep high-frequency actions and operational views easier to reach.
  • Use consistent naming and hierarchy across tools so navigation can scale as more capabilities are added.

Design principles

Context before action

Make the state of a system visible before users make changes that could affect game teams or platform operations.

One mental model

Reduce the feeling that every tool has its own rules by establishing shared navigation and information patterns.

Scale through structure

Create IA decisions that can absorb future platform capabilities without adding another layer of fragmentation.

Operational confidence

Help users know what is safe, what is blocked, and what requires another owner or prerequisite.

Impact

  • Provided a clearer product model for environment-related work across platform teams.
  • Reduced reliance on tribal knowledge by making status, ownership, and next actions more explicit.
  • Created navigation patterns that could support a broader internal platform ecosystem.
