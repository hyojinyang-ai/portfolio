Case 02 / Design Maturity Infrastructure

UX Research Bank & Design Logs

Principal UX Designer | Research Ops and Design Practice | 2023-Present

I created reusable research and design documentation practices so platform teams could make decisions from shared evidence instead of scattered notes, one-off workshop outputs, or memory.

Practice Problem

Insights were hard to find, hard to compare, and easy to lose once a project moved on. This made teams repeat discovery and revisit settled decisions.

Outcome

A more visible research and decision trail that helped PMs, engineers, and designers connect user evidence to product direction.

Problem

Artifact slot / UX research bank, research notes, opportunity map, or design log examples

In a complex platform environment, every team had fragments of user knowledge. Research summaries, stakeholder notes, usability findings, and design rationale existed, but not in a form that made them easy to retrieve or reuse.

This limited UX maturity in two ways: teams could not easily build on earlier learning, and design decisions were often judged as opinions because the evidence trail was invisible.

System I created

Research bank

A shared place to collect user insights, pain points, quotes, usability findings, and recurring themes across internal platform workstreams.

Design logs

A lightweight record of key decisions, rejected options, rationale, open questions, and the evidence behind a recommendation.

Reusable templates

Standard structures for research notes, synthesis, opportunity framing, and decision documentation, so quality did not depend on starting from scratch.

Evidence-to-roadmap loop

Findings were translated into product implications, making research useful for prioritization rather than only presentation.

Design decisions

1. Make the format light enough to use

The documentation model had to fit how platform teams actually worked. I kept it practical: enough structure to create consistency, not so much that it became another admin burden.

2. Separate evidence from interpretation

Research artifacts distinguished what users said or did from what the team inferred. This helped keep product debates grounded and reduced circular discussion.

3. Treat decisions as living product memory

Design logs captured why choices were made. That helped new team members understand context and prevented the same tradeoffs from being reopened without new evidence.

The work raised UX maturity by making research visible, decision-making explainable, and design rationale easier for cross-functional partners to trust.

Impact

  • Reduced duplicated discovery by making previous research easier to find and reuse.
  • Improved design critique quality because discussions could reference user evidence and decision history.
  • Created a scalable way for a small UX function to influence multiple platform workstreams.
  • Helped shift UX from delivery support toward strategic product learning.
